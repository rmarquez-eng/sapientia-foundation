Our Mission

Free, non-commercial financial education — and the tools to use it.

The Fair Credit & Debt Program is purely educational and self-directed. The Foundation charges consumers nothing, makes no loans, does not negotiate or settle debts on anyone's behalf, and neither pays nor receives commissions or referral fees. Our only interest is an informed, financially stable community.

Sapientia Foundation is an irrevocable non-profit charitable trust, organized under the laws of Delaware and recognized as a 501(c)(3) public charity. EIN 39-4961628.

Why This Matters

A damaged credit record is a quiet tax on the households least able to afford it.

A credit record follows a person into every major financial decision — renting a home, buying a car, getting a loan, setting an insurance premium, sometimes getting a job. Most people do not know their rights under the Fair Credit Reporting Act, and commercial "credit repair" charges high fees for work a person can do themselves once someone shows them how.
