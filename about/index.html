About the Foundation

A charitable trust established to advance education and economic opportunity — delivering direct community education rather than grantmaking.

Organization

Sapientia Foundation, A Charitable Trust

Sapientia Foundation is an irrevocable non-profit charitable trust, organized October 15, 2025 under the laws of Delaware and recognized as a 501(c)(3) public charity. Its Declaration of Trust expressly authorizes programs "for education or betterment" and "for economic relief or hardship."

The Foundation is governed by a Board of Trustees that meets at least quarterly and requires unanimous trustee consent for financial commitments. It delivers direct community education rather than making grants to other organizations.

Safeguards

How we stay accountable.

Not commercial credit repair

No consumer fees, no loans, no debt negotiation, no commissions or referral fees. 501(c)(3) organizations are excluded from the federal Credit Repair Organizations Act and most state credit-services-organization statutes; counsel confirms each state's treatment before the program operates there. The program is designed as education plus self-help to remain outside substantial "credit counseling" under IRC § 501(q).

Board independence

A conflict-of-interest policy, recusal process, and annual disclosure are adopted early in the program, with a majority of independent voting trustees.

Your data stays yours

No product sales, no lender or carrier sponsorship, no lead generation. Personal data is minimized, stored securely, and never shared with lenders, insurers, credit bureaus, or third parties.

Financial controls

Segregation of duties between the Managing Director and Secretary, dual approval for disbursements above $5,000, restricted-fund accounting, and an independent annual financial audit.
