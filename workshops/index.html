Workshops & clinics

Free and multilingual. In person with partner libraries, congregations, reentry and workforce programs, and legal-aid offices across Northern Virginia — and online for everyone else.

The workshop series

A short series, plain language, no sales pitch.

Each session stands on its own — come to one or all of them.

Reading your credit report and score

What is on a report, where it comes from, what actually moves a score, and what does not.

The Fair Credit Reporting Act and your right to dispute

What the law entitles you to, the timelines that apply, and what a valid dispute looks like.

Budgeting and debt payoff

Building a workable budget and choosing between avalanche, snowball, and consolidation.

Medical and student debt

Disputing medical bills, understanding recent medical-debt reporting changes, and student-loan status.

Collections and time limits

Debt validation, the statute of limitations, and time-barred debt.

Predatory loans and fees · building credit safely

Spotting high-cost lending, and building a thin file with secured cards and reporting rent.

Document-prep clinics

Bring your report. Leave with your letters drafted.

  1. Pull your reports

    An educator helps you request your reports from all three bureaus at no cost.

  2. Review them together

    Go line by line and flag anything inaccurate, unverifiable, or past the reporting window.

  3. Draft your letters

    Use the free tool to prepare your dispute, validation, and goodwill letters. You are the author and sender.

  4. Organize your follow-up

    Leave with a tracking sheet and the dates to watch for each response.
