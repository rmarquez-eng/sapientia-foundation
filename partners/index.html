Partner with us

You bring the space and the trust of your community. We bring the curriculum, the trained educators, and the free tools — plus train-the-trainer sessions so your own staff can carry the program forward.

Who we work with

Organizations already trusted by the people we serve.

Public libraries

Meeting rooms, standing audiences, and a neutral, no-cost setting.

Faith congregations

Trusted space and multilingual reach into immigrant and refugee communities.

Reentry & workforce programs

Participants rebuilding credit alongside employment and housing.

Housing counseling agencies

Clients who need report corrections before they can qualify.

Legal-aid organizations

Subject-matter review, and a referral path for participants who need licensed advice.

Community colleges & adult education

Young adults and returning students building a first credit file.

How it works

A clear division of labor.

Partners provide

Space for workshops and clinics, referrals from the community you already serve, and subject-matter review of curriculum where you have expertise.

Sapientia provides

Curriculum and participant materials, trained educators to deliver sessions, the free self-help tool and letter library, and train-the-trainer sessions so your staff can run the program independently.

No cost to partners or participants. No product sales, no lender or carrier sponsorship, no lead generation. Personal data is minimized, stored securely, and never shared with lenders, insurers, credit bureaus, or third parties.
Train the trainer

Run it yourself after one cycle.

  1. Co-deliver

    Sapientia educators run the first workshop series and clinic cycle with your staff observing and assisting.

  2. Certify

    Your staff complete the train-the-trainer session and receive the full curriculum, slides, and facilitator guides.

  3. Deliver independently

    Your organization runs the program on its own schedule, with Sapientia available for curriculum updates and hard questions.
