Credit Dispute Center

A guided walkthrough: enter your information, read your own credit report, classify what looks wrong, and generate your own dispute, validation, and goodwill letters. It tracks each response deadline and names the next letter if a response is missing or inadequate. Your case is stored only in this browser — you can export it as a file at any time.

How your data is handled. If you upload a credit report PDF, it is read once in memory to pull out the text and is never written to any database, file, or log. Your name, notes, and letters are stored only in your own browser. Nothing is shared with lenders, insurers, credit bureaus, or anyone else. See our Privacy Policy.
