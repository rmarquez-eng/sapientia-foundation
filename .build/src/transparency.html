Use of funds

Where money goes

Sapientia Foundation delivers direct community education rather than making grants. Contributions fund program staff and educators, curriculum development and translation, the self-help tool, workshop and clinic costs, participant materials, the digital library, outreach, evaluation, and required legal, audit, and insurance costs. No funds are used for trustee compensation for contributions to the trust, for lobbying, or for any political activity.
