Free guided self-help tool

Upload your own credit report. The tool reads it, flags entries that look inaccurate or out of date, and helps you prepare your own dispute, validation, and goodwill letters. You review, sign, and send every letter yourself.

Your report is never stored. The PDF is read once, in memory, to pull out the text — it is not written to any database, file, or log, and nothing is shared with lenders, insurers, credit bureaus, or third parties. Uploads are rate-limited to prevent abuse. This tool is educational and self-directed; it is not credit repair, and nothing here is legal advice.
