Who we are

Sapientia Foundation, A Charitable Trust

Sapientia Foundation is a charitable trust established in 2025 to advance education and economic opportunity. We are an organization described in Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code, governed by a volunteer Board of Trustees. We deliver free, plain-language financial education and self-help tools directly to the public and through a network of community partners. We are administered under the laws of Delaware and operate from Woodbridge, Virginia.

The trust is irrevocable. Its Declaration of Trust expressly authorizes programs "for education or betterment" and "for economic relief or hardship." The Board meets at least quarterly and requires unanimous trustee consent for financial commitments.

Mission

Educating people to understand credit, debt, and money, to correct the errors that hold them back, and to build lasting economic stability in communities the system overlooks.

Values

What guides the work

Free, always

Every workshop, clinic, tool, and document is free to the people who use it. No fees, no products, no upsell.

Plain language

Financial rights explained the way you would explain them to a friend — in multiple languages.

Self-directed

The participant reads their own report, writes their own letters, and keeps their own records. We teach; we do not act on anyone's behalf.

Open

Curriculum and tools are released under an open license so any library, clinic, or legal-aid office can deliver the program themselves.

Non-commercial statement. Sapientia Foundation charges no fees, sells no products, makes no loans, negotiates no debts on anyone's behalf, and pays or receives no commissions or referral fees. All of our materials are free and released under an open license so any organization may copy, print, translate, and share them.

Governance & accountability

The Foundation is governed by a volunteer Board of Trustees and is recruiting additional independent trustees and a volunteer advisory council.
